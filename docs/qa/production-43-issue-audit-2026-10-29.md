### 1.2 Top Critical Risks Identified on Production

1. **Catalog Sorting Price Inversion (Issue #3 & #4):**
   - *Observation:* On `/search?sort=price_asc`, product card prices rendered in non-monotonic sequence: `[$0.25, $0.30, $0.30, $0.30, $0.35, $0.70, $1.00, $0.80]`. The `$1.00` item appeared before the `$0.80` item.
   - *Mechanism:* The backend database query (`ProductController.php:324`) orders by scalar column `wholesale_price ASC`. However, storefront cards calculate their active display price dynamically via `getLowestValidCustomerUnitPrice(product)` across Standard, Bulk, and Full Stock tiers. Products with low base wholesale prices but high tier minimums sort ahead of products with lower active unit prices.
   - *Risk:* Severe merchant credibility risk; wholesale buyers searching for cheapest lots encounter misordered prices.

2. **False-Positive Anti-Bot Honeypot Triggers (Issue #10 & #13):**
   - *Observation:* Wholesale RFQ and Order Confirmation submissions fail with the banner `"Automated form submission detected."`
   - *Mechanism:* `HoneypotField.tsx` injects a hidden input with `name="website_url_hp"` and `autoComplete="new-password"`. Modern browser autofill engines (Chrome Autofill, 1Password, LastPass) mistakenly inject stored credentials into this hidden input. Both `rfq.service.ts:147` and `NativeBotProtectionService.php:84` reject any non-empty honeypot value with a 422 error. Furthermore, legitimate buyers using pre-filled forms trigger the rapid submission guard (`$elapsed < 2s`).
   - *Risk:* Direct commercial revenue loss; legitimate international buyers cannot submit RFQs or complete checkout.

3. **Inconsistent Modal Backdrop Outside-Click Dismissal (Issues #12, #14, #27, #33):**
   - *Observation:* Multiple critical dialogs (Sign Out modal, Order Confirmation modal, Admin Coupon Edit modal, and Administrator Creation modal) fail to dismiss when clicking the backdrop overlay.
   - *Mechanism:* Several modal components rely on `e.target === e.currentTarget` on a scrollable outer wrapper. When the wrapper contains padding or sub-pixel margins, `e.target` hits pseudo-elements or inner flex containers, preventing `onClose()` from firing. Conversely, modals with explicit backdrop layers (`AdminUserModal.tsx`) pass cleanly.
   - *Risk:* Frustrating user experience; trapped users must find tiny 'X' or 'Cancel' buttons.

## 4. In-Depth Root Cause Analysis for Failed Issues

### Issue #3 & #4: Catalog Sorting Non-Monotonicity and Alphabetical Distortion
- **Location:**
  - Backend Query: [`ProductController.php#L324-L365`](file:///Users/labibhasan/Documents/AYC/backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/V1/ProductController.php#L324-L365)
  - Frontend Display: [`ProductCard.tsx#L58-L70`](file:///Users/labibhasan/Documents/AYC/src/components/product/ProductCard.tsx#L58-L70)
- **Root Cause Mechanism:**
  When a customer selects `Price: Low to High` (`sort=price_asc`), the backend SQL query orders by `wholesale_price ASC`. However, on AYC, products feature tiered pricing structures (Standard MOQ tier, Bulk MOQ tier, Full Stock tier). The product card renders the price calculated by `getLowestValidCustomerUnitPrice(product)`. If Product 101 has `wholesale_price = $0.50` but its lowest active tier unit price is `$1.00`, and Product 102 has `wholesale_price = $0.80` with tier price `$0.80`, the database orders Product 101 first. On screen, the customer sees `$1.00` rendered ahead of `$0.80`.
